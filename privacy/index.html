Privacy policy: Chapchik

Last updated 28 November 2026.

Chapchik is a personal assistant used only by its author, Emil Valeev. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

It connects to the author's own Google account to read mail and calendar events and, only on the author's confirmation, to send mail or create calendar events.

Data from Google stays on the author's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Text of messages may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) to produce summaries for the author.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
